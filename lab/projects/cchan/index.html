Normalization cohort & formula

This editable sample plus three fixed fictional samples form a four-sample teaching cohort. All weights are recalculated together. This is not the research training set.

Λᵢ = yᵢ[λCcheck·pᵢ + Camount,ᵢ(1−pᵢ)] + (1−yᵢ)Ccheck·pᵢ

Λ̄ᵢ = Λᵢ / mean(Λ); lᵢ = Λ̄ᵢ · BCEᵢ

Teaching constants: Ccheck=280.9 cost units, λ=0.8. Exact endpoint logarithms are clamped at −100. A zero normalization mean makes weighted loss undefined.

Scope, graph rules & source differences

The paper is the chosen teaching reference. PH, PD and HD represent observed entity pairs, linked to basic entities shared by both constituents in the basic graph. PP displays patient pairs with a shared hospital or disease; isolated PP pairs are omitted for readability. No HH/DD expansion is implemented.

Shared-neighbor rules use an aggregated graph; they do not preserve timestamps or guarantee that every inferred context belongs to one original event. The public notebook creates HD nodes per patient and uses patient-specific semantic attention, unlike the paper’s shared-context/global-attention presentation. This page deliberately follows the paper as requested.

Paper Savings is 1−Cost/min(Cost_all,Cost_none), distinct from raw Cost Avoided. It is not presented as a research metric here. Ties sort by anonymous ID. Replay and direct prefix evaluation use identical cost arrays. No real local graph or causal explanation is fabricated.

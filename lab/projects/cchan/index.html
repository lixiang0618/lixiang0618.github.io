The Hidden Cost of Risk-Only Decisions

Prioritizing patients solely by fraud probability may allocate excessive investigation resources to high-risk but low-value claims, while overlooking moderately risky cases involving substantial financial exposure. Unnecessary inspections and costly missed fraud cases create two competing sources of economic loss.

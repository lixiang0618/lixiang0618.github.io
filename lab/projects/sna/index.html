From Gravity to Network

Institutional scale, resource conditions and transportation friction.

Newtonian gravity

F = G m₁ m₂r²

F: gravitational force · G: gravitational constant · m₁, m₂: object masses · r: distance between objects.

Spatial interaction

Iᵢⱼ = K Mᵢ Mⱼdᵢⱼβ

Iᵢⱼ: interaction intensity · K: proportionality constant · Mᵢ, Mⱼ: location scale · dᵢⱼ: spatial distance · β: distance-decay exponent · i, j: location indices.

Physical mass → Institutional scale　·　Force → Modeled attraction　·　Distance → Transportation friction. A conceptual adaptation, not a physical derivation.

The modified gravity model · Eq. 1.3

Fᵢⱼ = LSᵢLSᵢ + LSⱼ · Qᵢ QⱼDᵢⱼ
Qᵢ = ⁴√PCAi · RCi · BGi · NSi
Dᵢⱼ = Σₘ aₘ Tₘ Mₘ

Qⱼ follows the same expression with j in place of i. Q and D abbreviate the report’s expanded equation without changing it.

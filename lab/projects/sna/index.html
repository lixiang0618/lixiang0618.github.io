Core fit is not a strongly connected component

The saved core–periphery fit correlation is 0.9723. Fitting a core–periphery pattern and finding mutual directed reachability are different methods. Their 11-node memberships coincide here, verified from the binary matrix; this is not a general equivalence. The matrix has 15 strongly connected components but one weakly connected component, not 15 disconnected weak components.

04

Network Microscope

Structural holes, shortest-path centrality and brokerage are distinct lenses.

→ Outgoing← Incoming↔ Reciprocal

Structural holes describe nonredundant connections and constraint. Betweenness counts shortest-path mediation. Brokerage classifies directed open triads by group membership. These are not interchangeable indicators. Local faint edges show existing neighbor relations, not measured resource transfers.

05

Show the working.

Transparent assumptions matter more than convincing numbers.

Motion synthesis

Seeded piecewise position trajectories → numerical velocity → numerical acceleration + gravity + small sensor noise. Walking uses asymmetric gait templates; push-ups and squats have different depths, timing and pauses; running and frog jumps include ballistic flight and landing. Sitting uses slow nonperiodic posture drift.

The world-aligned traces are teaching approximations, not raw wearable IMU measurements.

X ∈ ℝT × 6 = [ax, ay, az, vx, vy, vz]

CNN architecture, without a trained CNN

Signals → feature extraction → 1D CNN architecture demonstration → six rule-based logits → stable Softmax → MET lookup. The fixed convolution and pooling illustrate architecture; classification rules independently use signal features.

Food and portions

Nportion=N100g×w100

Preset selection → simulated classification → nutrient example → portion scaling → daily intake. Preparation state matters; a photograph cannot determine portion weight. Missing values stay unknown and make totals explicitly partial.

Daily comparison and privacy

Edifference = Efood − Eactive

Food intake + exercise expenditure → local aggregation → daily comparison. Macronutrients, sugar and food water show intake only. Local records do not sync across devices. This is an educational demo, not medical or dietary advice.
